Separately, this project's jobs figure was reconciled **down** to 27 FT (the consistently-cited incentive-basis figure) from a previously-carried ~150 company estimate.
- **Wolfspeed's Aug. 19 Q4 FY26 earnings call delivered a genuinely mixed signal for Siler City, NC.** The company's Siler City capacity expansion reached "production readiness," but underutilization costs kept climbing ($47M in Q4, up from $26M in Q1 FY26) as customer demand has not caught up to installed capacity; FY26 revenue grew 138% YoY but the company posted a $415.8M full-year net loss. The previously-flagged "unverified layoffs headline" is now resolved as referencing stale 2025-dated WARN notices, not a new 2026 action.

**What happened:** Two developments cut in opposite directions this cycle. Positive: Gov. McMaster's Aug. 17, 2026 budget veto strikes a provision that would have required separate legislative approval before the state could pay a $150M cost overrun at the Scout site (pending a December 2026 audit) — McMaster argued withholding the money was "bad business policy," clearing the funding to flow. ## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (new deals score low; repeated/gaining-momentum deals score higher) |

**Score sensitivity to data quality:** A milestone that measurably reduces execution risk (Genentech's topping-out ceremony; Octapharma's public site-plan reveal) moves the score up modestly even without a full construction start or closing. Offsetting developments (Scout Motors' funding veto vs. community-relations friction) can net to zero rather than forcing an artificial up or down move — the methodology does not require every recheck to produce a score change.
