# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-08-10 (Week 4)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-02-10 → 2026-08-10** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle/Triad, Upstate SC, Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note — read first, includes a material coverage gap.** This is Week 4, built on the 2026-07-13 report (a four-week gap since the last cycle). Five parallel research passes were assigned to cover Charlotte metro, the NC Triangle, the NC Triad/WNC/Coast, Upstate SC, and SC Midlands/Lowcountry. **Charlotte metro, the NC Triangle, and the NC Triad/WNC/Coast passes completed thoroughly** (~40, ~38, and ~30 searches respectively). **The Upstate SC pass ran only 4 of its planned ~25 searches, and the SC Midlands/Lowcountry pass ran zero searches**, because this session's web-search tool budget (a hard 200-call session-wide cap shared across all five research passes) was exhausted by the time those two agents started, and this sandbox's network egress policy separately blocks direct page fetches to nearly every news/government domain used by this report (confirmed via the proxy's own status endpoint returning 403 policy denials for postandcourier.com, governor.sc.gov, scdailygazette.com, and others). **Practical effect:** every SC Midlands/Lowcountry entry below (Scout Motors' Blythewood plant, the SC Ports Leatherman Terminal pause, AMAROK, Ferrara Candy, Boeing 787, HII, Google) is carried forward from the 2026-07-13 report **unverified this cycle** and explicitly flagged as such — none of it is invented, but none of it is freshly confirmed either. Several Upstate SC watch items are in the same position. This is the report's single biggest open item for next cycle; see the flagged rows below and the Week-over-Week section. No numbers were invented anywhere in this report; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.

**Key takeaways for a MF/townhome developer:**

- **Octapharma is now a closed deal, not a pending one.** All three governmental approvals (York County, Rock Hill, and the state's Closing Fund grant) are in place. Model Rock Hill/Fort Mill absorption against a real, de-risked $1.5B project — construction is expected to start "as early as next year" (2027).
- **Charlotte adds a second Scout Motors entry.** The automaker's global HQ (Plaza Midwood, 1,200 jobs, $153,978 stated wage) is distinct from its Blythewood, SC assembly plant — both are now separately tracked. The HQ's second-building land-development filing is real forward movement, though only Medium-confidence (a preliminary filing, not a permit or groundbreaking).
- **JetZero has moved from "funded and ceremonially broken ground" to "actual construction contractor on site."** HITT Contracting is now general contractor on the first phase (NC Composite Center); this reduces execution-risk uncertainty even though the 2037 full-hiring-ramp target is unchanged.
- **Two negative reversals hit the NC Triangle this cycle:** Chatham County commissioners voted to **terminate VinFast's $400M incentive agreement** (2026-07-27 to 07-31) after the company missed its scaled-back hiring/investment thresholds, and NC Commerce **cancelled Kempower's JDIG** in Durham/RTP (~mid-July 2026) after the EV-charger maker fell far short of its 306-job pledge. Neither was ever Ranked-tier, but both are real downside data points for their submarkets.
- **The report's biggest unresolved question this cycle is whether SC Ports actually paused Leatherman Terminal operations on 2026-08-01 as announced on 2026-06-25** — today is 2026-08-10, so the pause should already be in effect, but this cycle's tool-budget exhaustion prevented confirmation. This is the #1 priority recheck for next cycle, alongside the rest of the SC Midlands/Lowcountry region.
- **Boom Supersonic's risk continues to escalate with no offsetting signal.** ~19 open roles as of August 2026 is nowhere near the hiring surge needed to hit 500 employees by the Dec. 31, 2026 deadline, which is now under five months away.

### 2. Rock Hill / York County — Octapharma "Project Palmetto Rock" (York, SC) — Score 90 — Updated (resolved)
**What happened:** Rock Hill City Council voted **unanimously, 7-0, on 2026-07-23**, to approve the amended tax-share terms that York County Council had adopted on a narrower 4-3 vote on 2026-07-08. Council members publicly criticized York County's late changes to the incentive structure even while approving it. Separately, the SC Coordinating Council for Economic Development awarded a **$65,000,000 Closing Fund grant to York County** for site prep, building construction, and road improvements. By 2026-07-29/07-31, multiple outlets (WFAE, SC Daily Gazette) describe the $1.5B deal as fully "finalized"/"inked." Land closing is expected "in the coming months," with construction "as early as next year" (2027) and operations targeted for the mid-2030s.
**Why it matters:** This resolves the single largest open execution risk this dataset has tracked for six weeks running — a genuinely closed deal is a materially stronger housing-demand signal than a conditionally-approved one.
**Housing-demand implication:** Unchanged in scale (dual-tier: $141k HQ roles support luxury for-sale/Class-A; $102k manufacturing roles support premium workforce-plus rental/townhome product in Rock Hill/Fort Mill), now backed by meaningfully higher deal certainty.
**Source:** [Fort Mill Sun, 2026-07-23](https://www.fortmillsun.com/2026/07/23/rock-hill-council-clears-octapharma-deal-in-7-0-votes-as-members-blast-york-county-over-incentive-fight/) · [WBTV, 2026-07-23](https://www.wbtv.com/2026/07/23/rock-hills-octapharma-projects-moved-forward-what-we-know/) · [Governor McMaster's office, 2026-07](https://governor.sc.gov/news/2026-07/octapharma-selects-york-county-its-first-us-manufacturing-site) · [WFAE, 2026-07-29](https://www.wfae.org/business/2026-07-29/octapharma-finalizes-1-5-billion-rock-hill-project-bringing-1-500-jobs) · [SC Daily Gazette, 2026-07-31](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/07/31/rock-hill-inks-1-5-billion-investment-deal-with-pharmaceutical-company-for-former-panthers-site/)

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (new deals score low; repeated/gaining-momentum deals score higher) |

**Score sensitivity to data quality:** a fully resolved deal (Octapharma's unanimous city-council approval) removes a project-certainty discount and moves the score up meaningfully; a milestone that is real but early-stage (Scout Motors HQ's preliminary land-development filing) caps the score below deals with permits or construction underway; an item this cycle could not independently reverify (Scout Motors Blythewood) is carried forward at its last-known score rather than guessed at.

## Appendix: This Cycle's Coverage Gap (see methodology note in full at top)

This session's web-search tool budget is a **hard 200-call cap shared across the entire session**, including all five regional research passes run in parallel. By the time the Upstate SC and SC Midlands/Lowcountry passes started, the budget was already exhausted (Charlotte metro alone used ~99 tool calls; the NC Triangle and NC Triad/WNC/Coast passes used ~78 and ~53). Separately, this sandbox's network egress policy blocks direct page fetches (not just search) to nearly every news and government domain this report relies on — confirmed via 403 policy-denial responses from the proxy for postandcourier.com, governor.sc.gov, and scdailygazette.com, among others — so there was no fallback path once the search budget ran out. **Net effect: the SC Midlands/Lowcountry region (Richland, Lexington, Charleston, Berkeley, Dorchester counties) received zero fresh research this cycle, and Upstate SC (Greenville, Spartanburg, Anderson, Cherokee, Laurens, Pickens counties) received only 4 of its planned ~25 searches.** Every SC Midlands/Lowcountry and unrechecked Upstate SC entry in this report is explicitly labeled "not rechecked this cycle" and carries forward its last-verified figures rather than any new or invented data. Recommend either raising the session search budget or splitting future runs across multiple sessions so each region gets its own budget allocation.
