# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-10-26 (Week 15)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-04-26 → 2026-10-26** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note — read first.** This is Week 15, built on the 2026-10-19 report. All five regional research passes ran this cycle (Charlotte metro, NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry) via dedicated parallel research agents, each re-verifying region-specific Ranked/Watch entries and searching broadly for new qualifying announcements. Findings rely on WebSearch-result content/snippets and WebFetch page retrieval, which do surface real, retrievable source URLs, cross-checked across multiple independent queries per claim. No numbers were invented anywhere in this report; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.
>
> **Window note:** the trailing 6-month window advanced from 2026-04-19→10-19 to **2026-04-26→10-26**. No Ranked entry's sole milestone aged out of window this cycle. No Watch-tier item was removed this cycle; GNQ Insilico (Greenville, SC) had its location-confidence downgraded to Low pending a sourcing recheck next cycle (recent company sourcing ties it to Pleasanton, CA / Ontario, not SC).
