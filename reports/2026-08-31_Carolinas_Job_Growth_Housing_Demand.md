> **Methodology note — read first.** This is Week 7, built on the 2026-08-24 report. All five regional research passes ran this cycle (Charlotte metro, NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry) via dedicated parallel research agents, each re-verifying every region-specific Ranked/Watch entry and searching broadly for new qualifying announcements. **WebFetch (direct page retrieval) again returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED` for essentially every news/government domain attempted this cycle**, regardless of agent; every finding below relies on WebSearch-result content and snippets, which do surface real, retrievable source URLs, cross-checked across multiple independent queries per claim. Each region's WebSearch budget was exhausted before every conceivable follow-up query could be run; gaps are flagged explicitly where they affected a finding (see §1 and the per-item notes). No numbers were invented anywhere in this report; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction, or already open) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (new deals score low; repeated/gaining-momentum deals score higher) |

**Score sensitivity to data quality:** A milestone that measurably reduces execution risk (AbbVie's confirmed county incentive; Citigroup's already-completed grand opening) moves the score up modestly even without a full construction start. New Ranked-tier entries are capped by zero repeat-momentum on their first appearance regardless of how de-risked the underlying facts are.
