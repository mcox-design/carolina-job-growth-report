# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-10-12 (Week 13)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-04-12 → 2026-10-12** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note — read first.** This is Week 13, built on the 2026-10-05 report. All five regional research passes ran this cycle (Charlotte metro, NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry) via dedicated parallel research agents, each re-verifying region-specific Ranked/Watch entries and searching broadly for new qualifying announcements. The main research session supplemented these with its own direct checks on two items that fell outside any single agent's assigned scope (Octapharma, Rock Hill SC; the SC Ports Leatherman Terminal status). **WebFetch (direct page retrieval) again returned blocked/DNS-failure/egress-restricted for the large majority of news/government/company/SEC domains attempted this cycle**, regardless of agent; nearly every finding below relies on WebSearch-result content and snippets, which do surface real, retrievable source URLs, cross-checked across multiple independent queries per claim. No numbers were invented anywhere in this report; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.
>
> **Window note:** the trailing 6-month window advanced from 2026-04-05→10-05 to **2026-04-12→10-12**. No Ranked entry's sole milestone aged out of window this cycle. One Watch-tier item (Siemens Energy's $421M statewide expansion) was removed after its sole milestone aged fully outside the window with no in-window update found; one further Watch item (Siemens AG power-devices, Raleigh/Wendell/Knightdale) is now also just outside the window and flagged for recheck or likely removal next cycle.

**Key takeaways for a MF/townhome developer:**

- **The quiet multi-cycle stretch for brand-new Ranked-tier finds continues, and this cycle also saw zero Ranked-tier score movement** — a first in several cycles. All five regional research passes, plus two direct follow-up checks, found no new 500+ job/$100k+ wage company announcement with an in-window milestone anywhere in the Carolinas.
- **The most consequential developments this cycle were legal/regulatory, not fundamentals-moving.** The Spartanburg, SC NorthMark/Valara AI/HPC data-center lawsuit (Watch tier, $2.8–3.0B) was **DISMISSED** on procedural grounds 2026-10-06 — plaintiffs' harm claims were ruled too generalized and they'd failed to exhaust administrative remedies before suing — materially easing litigation risk for the developer, though SELC says it will appeal and a second permit-application fight remains open. Separately, VinFast's Chatham County litigation escalated into an adversarial posture: VinFast filed an ~80-page response blaming the *state's* failure to cooperate for the delays and seeking damages, rather than simply defending. And Scout Motors filed a motion in the Shives-family lawsuit (Blythewood, SC) seeking to shift liability for alleged home damage onto its subcontractors.
- **One positive confirmed-opening milestone:** Amazon's Pender/New Hanover robotics fulfillment center — its largest in NC — is now confirmed **OPEN** (~1,100 employees at opening, per an Oct. 6 WECT report), though the wage remains Estimated/Inferred at ~$22/hr, keeping it at Watch rather than Ranked.
- **Boom Supersonic's Greensboro situation worsened further.** AI-infrastructure firm Crusoe terminated a $1.25B turbine-supply order from Boom's power-generation spinoff on 9/25/26, a real financial headwind just as Boom's Dec. 31, 2026 deadline to reach 500 Greensboro-area employees (or risk lease termination) draws inside 3 months, with no confirmed site-specific headcount found this cycle.
- **Two new Watch-tier items**, both in the Fountain Inn, SC submarket (straddling Greenville/Laurens counties): EPC Power Corp's AI-data-center power-inverter plant (275 jobs, opened ~7/15/26, now being acquired by Flex for $4.4B) and a $180M Prisma Health medical campus (jobs undisclosed) — together suggesting an emerging growth corridor worth tracking. **One item was removed from Watch:** Siemens Energy's $421M statewide expansion, after a dedicated recheck found no in-window milestone and its sole (Feb. 2026) announcement date aged fully outside the new window.
