# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-07-02 (Week 2)

**Coverage window:** Announcements dated **2026-01-02 → 2026-07-02** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle/Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note (read first).** This is Week 2, following the inaugural 2026-06-24 baseline report. The window rolled forward ~8 days (now 2026-01-02 → 2026-07-02), so the entire prior baseline set remains in scope with no items rolling out the back edge. Research this week combined three parallel regional passes (Charlotte metro; NC Triangle/Triad/WNC/Coastal; Upstate SC + SC Midlands/Lowcountry), each running 15–45 web searches against primary and near-primary sources. **Tooling caveat:** the direct-fetch (WebFetch) tool returned HTTP 403 on every domain tested this week, including non-protected test domains — this is a site/bot-protection pattern, not a proxy failure (confirmed against proxy status logs). All findings below therefore rest on web-search-engine result content (which surfaces real, verifiable source URLs and substantive snippets) rather than a second-pass raw-HTML fetch. Confidence ratings are held to "Medium" wherever a figure could not be cross-confirmed across two or more independent outlets. No numbers were invented; unstated figures are marked **"Not disclosed"** and inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis given.

## 1. Executive Summary

**Top markets this week (by composite score):**

1. **Charlotte / Uptown (Mecklenburg, NC) — SMBC Group, Score 94.** The 2,000-job, $165,316-average-salary bank-operations hub is now backed by a signed ~200,000 SF sublease at 301 S. College St (former Wachovia/One Wells Fargo Center), confirmed 2026-05-04 — the largest Mecklenburg jobs announcement since 2020 is converting from commitment to physical occupancy.
2. **Rock Hill / Palmetto Research Park (York, SC) — Octapharma, Score 90 — NEW.** The single biggest find of the week: a $1.49B biopharma HQ + manufacturing campus at the former Carolina Panthers HQ site — 1,252 new jobs (564 at $141,502 avg / 688 at $102,752 avg) plus 300 roles relocating from Charlotte. York County Council passed two unanimous incentive votes (6/15 and 6/29); the company was publicly named just three days before this report's cutoff.
3. **Blythewood / North Columbia (Richland, SC) — Scout Motors, Score 85.** No new milestone this week, but the April hiring-ramp/training-center wins (600+ hired toward 4,000) remain the strongest active EV-manufacturing engine on the I-77 corridor and stay in the window.
4. **Durham / RTP (Durham, NC) — AbbVie, Score 84.** Unchanged from last week — $1.4B biopharma campus, 734 jobs at $118,041 average, still the Triangle's premier high-wage anchor.
5. **Durham / Wake (Morrisville) — Novartis, Score 82 — NEW.** A fresh April 30 "seventh new facility" announcement (part of Novartis's $23B national plan) adds a $220M Morrisville API building to the existing Durham/Wake commitment, bringing the total to 700+ jobs at a **newly stated $111,161 average** — this project graduates from last week's Excluded list into Ranked.
6. **Charlotte (Mecklenburg, NC) — Capital Group, Score 81 — Updated.** The previously *inferred* ~$190k wage is now a **stated $194,141** (per JDIG filing) — the highest confirmed average wage of any deal in the report — plus a signed 196,940 SF lease at One Independence Center (5/28/26).
7. **Holly Springs / SW Wake (NC) — Genentech, Score 80.** Unchanged; the January investment-doubling to ~$2B remains in window.
8. **Greensboro / PTI Airport (Guilford, NC) — JetZero, Score 74 — Updated (delay).** Groundbreaking is real and happened 6/15/26, but the state confirmed JetZero's hiring-timeline slipped a full year (target extended from 12/31/2026 to 12/31/2027) amid a state-budget holdup; scored down from 82 to reflect that execution-timeline discount.
9. **Ballantyne (Charlotte, NC) — Citigroup, Score 73 — NEW (via milestone).** Last week's report excluded Citigroup's 510-job Ballantyne office as "grand opening tied to a prior-year commitment." On reflection, that in-window (2026-03-16) ribbon-cutting is a legitimate hiring-ramp milestone — the same standard already applied to Scout Motors and JetZero — so it is added this week with a stated $131,832 average wage.

**Key takeaways for a MF/townhome developer:**

- **The SC side of the Charlotte metro just got dramatically more interesting.** Octapharma's $1.49B Rock Hill campus is the largest single find since this report began, and it sits on the same 209-acre former-Panthers tract where Novant Health is separately building a $300M medical campus (255 jobs) — together the single biggest submarket redevelopment story in the report.
- **Charlotte's finance/finance-tech core keeps compounding.** SMBC and Capital Group both converted from "announced" to "leased" this week, and Citigroup's Ballantyne office is now open — three separate six-figure-average banking/finance hubs are simultaneously ramping in and around Uptown/South End/Ballantyne.
- **The Triangle's life-sciences cluster gained a fourth anchor.** AbbVie, Genentech, and now Novartis (newly in-window via its April "seventh facility" announcement) all carry stated six-figure average wages — this is the deepest, most repeatable high-income cluster in either state.
- **JetZero is real but slower.** Groundbreaking happened, but the state's own budget process pushed JetZero's hiring ramp back a full year — model Greensboro/PTI demand on a 2028+ timeline, not 2027.
- **A cancellation and a lawsuit are notable negative signals.** TigerDC's $3B Spartanburg data-center project was formally withdrawn after the county council denied its tax incentive (2/27/26), and the state of North Carolina sued VinFast (Chatham County) over missed construction/hiring deadlines, with VinFast's own hiring plan cut from 7,500 to ~1,400 jobs. Neither drives housing demand; both are logged for pipeline-risk awareness.
- **NC's stated-wage discipline continues to make it the easier state to underwrite.** Every NC deal in the Ranked table now carries a stated average wage (SMBC, AbbVie, Novartis, Capital Group, Genentech, JetZero, Citigroup). Octapharma is the first SC deal to buck the pattern with two clearly stated wage bands ($141,502 / $102,752) from its incentive filing — still the exception, not the rule, for South Carolina.

## 3. Market Writeups

### 1. Charlotte / Uptown — SMBC Group (Mecklenburg, NC) — Score 94
**What happened:** SMBC's 2,000-job, $165,316-average bank-operations hub converted from commitment to real estate this week — a ~200,000 SF sublease at 301 S. College St (the former Wachovia/One Wells Fargo Center) was confirmed 2026-05-04, with occupancy targeted fall 2026.
**Why it matters:** This remains the largest single Mecklenburg jobs announcement since 2020, and a signed lease materially de-risks the timeline.
**Housing-demand implication:** Strong, urban-concentrated. Expect near-term Uptown/South End/Dilworth luxury multifamily absorption as the six-year hiring ramp (2028–2032) begins staffing up ahead of the fall 2026 move-in.
**Source:** [NC Commerce, 2026-04-07](https://www.commerce.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2026/04/07/governor-stein-announces-2000-new-jobs-japans-smbc-group-selects-charlotte-bank-operations-expansion) · [Axios Charlotte, 2026-05-04 (lease)](https://www.axios.com/local/charlotte/2026/05/04/smbc-headquarters-wells-fargo-center-uptown)

### 2. Rock Hill / Palmetto Research Park — Octapharma (York, SC) — Score 90 — NEW
**What happened:** Octapharma (parent of the Charlotte-based Octapharma Plasma) is locating a new US HQ/lab and manufacturing campus — codename "Project Palmetto Rock" — at the former Carolina Panthers HQ/practice-facility site (I-77 Exit 81). The deal is **1,252 new jobs** (564 HQ/lab roles at a stated **$141,502 average** + 688 manufacturing roles at a stated **$102,752 average**) plus **300 jobs relocating** from the existing Charlotte subsidiary, on **$1.49 billion** in investment ($190M HQ/lab + $1.29B manufacturing). York County Council passed first reading 7-0 on 2026-06-15 and second reading 7-0 on 2026-06-29 — the meeting where the company was publicly identified for the first time.
**Why it matters:** This is the largest and highest-wage single find of the week and the first SC deal in this report to carry two clearly *stated* wage bands rather than an inference. It also anchors a broader 209-acre redevelopment of Rock Hill's highest-profile vacant tract (see Novant Health, Markets to Watch).
**Housing-demand implication:** Strong and cross-border. Blended wages of $103K–$141.5K support both Class-A rental and for-sale demand in Rock Hill/York County, and the 300 relocating Charlotte staff imply some household movement across the state line within the same metro.
**Caveat:** Construction could start as soon as 2027, with a ~10-year build-out; a third/final county vote is still expected, so treat as high-confidence-but-not-yet-fully-binding.
**Source:** [WRHI, 2026-06 (public identification)](https://www.wrhi.com/2026/06/global-biopharmaceutical-company-eyes-rock-hill-site-for-1-5-billion-facility-and-1252-jobs-213471) · [WRHI, 2026-06-29 (2nd reading)](https://www.wrhi.com/2026/06/york-county-council-passes-second-reading-of-1-5-billion-biopharma-deal-data-center-moratorium-213788) · [Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/york-county/news/rock-hill-plasma-panthers-octapharma-billion/article_fbd570b1-6451-406b-bb8c-92a53fad0d7c.html) · [QC News](https://www.qcnews.com/news/u-s/york-county/biopharmaceutical-company-revealed-that-could-create-1200-jobs-in-rock-hill/)

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction, leased) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports; confirmed milestones (lease signings, groundbreakings) score higher than static repeats; confirmed delays are discounted |

**Score sensitivity to data quality:** inferred wages cap the "salary" factor below a stated equivalent (this is why Octapharma, despite stated wages, sits just below SMBC — its deal isn't fully finalized yet, discounting "project certainty"); confirmed timeline delays (JetZero) discount "project certainty" further this week than last.
