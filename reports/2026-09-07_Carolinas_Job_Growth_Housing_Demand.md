### Week of 2026-09-07 (Week 8)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-03-07 → 2026-09-07** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note — read first.** This is Week 8, built on the 2026-08-31 report. All five regional research passes ran this cycle (Charlotte metro, NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry) via dedicated parallel research agents, each re-verifying every region-specific Ranked/Watch entry and searching broadly for new qualifying announcements, plus a targeted follow-up pass by the report compiler to resolve the Scout Motors HQ filing date, the VinFast litigation deadline, and the SELC injunction status. **WebFetch (direct page retrieval) again returned blocked/`EGRESS_BLOCKED` for essentially every news/government domain attempted this cycle**, regardless of agent; every finding below relies on WebSearch-result content and snippets, which do surface real, retrievable source URLs, cross-checked across multiple independent queries per claim. Each region's WebSearch budget was exhausted before every conceivable follow-up query could be run; gaps are flagged explicitly where they affected a finding. No numbers were invented anywhere in this report; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.
>
> **Window shift note:** the trailing 6-month window advanced exactly one week (2026-02-28→08-31 to **2026-03-07→09-07**), which pushed a handful of Watch-tier items' sole dated milestone just outside the window (Ralliant Corp's 2026-03-05 grand opening; Environmental Air Systems' 2025-11-25 announcement; Ahold Delhaize's 2026-02-16 groundbreaking). Consistent with this report's standing-item convention (as already applied to Google's Berkeley/Dorchester data center and Redwood Materials, both now in a 6th consecutive quiet cycle), these are flagged **"at risk"** in the Watch tier rather than dropped outright, since each was actively rechecked this cycle with no fresher update found.
