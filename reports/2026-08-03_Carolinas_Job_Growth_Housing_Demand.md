# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-08-03 (Week 4)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-02-03 → 2026-08-03** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle/Triad, Upstate SC, Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note (read first).** This is Week 4, built on the 2026-07-13 report (a 3-week gap — the prior cycle ran through 2026-07-13). Five parallel research passes covered Charlotte metro, the NC Triangle, the NC Triad/WNC/Coast, Upstate SC, and SC Midlands/Lowcountry, each explicitly re-checking every prior-week ranked and watch-tier entry for in-window updates before searching for new candidates. **No brand-new qualifying (500+ jobs / $100k+ wage) deal was found in any of the five sub-regions this cycle** — all 8 ranked entries from the 2026-07-13 report carry forward, with one major resolution (Octapharma) and continued momentum on two others. No numbers were invented; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated. WebFetch was unreliable during research (frequent HTTP 403s from the sandbox's outbound proxy on individual news-site domains); all cited findings are grounded in retrieved/quoted web-search content rather than fabricated, and every URL below was returned by a live search against the real page.

**Top markets this week (by composite score):**

5. **Charlotte (Mecklenburg, NC) — Capital Group, Score 81 (unchanged).** No new milestone this cycle; hiring doesn't begin until 2027.
6. **Holly Springs / SW Wake (NC) — Genentech, Score 80 (unchanged).** No new milestone this cycle.
7. **Greensboro / PTI Airport (Guilford, NC) — JetZero, Score 80 (Gaining Momentum, +1).** JetZero and the U.S. Export-Import Bank signed a Letter of Interest on 2026-07-20 to explore up to **$3B** in EXIM financing for the Greensboro campus — a capital-durability signal, not a jobs/wage change. The hiring ramp still begins 2028-2029 per the prior JDIG amendment.
8. **Durham / Morrisville (Durham & Wake, NC) — Novartis, Score 77 (unchanged).** No new milestone this cycle.

**Key takeaways for a MF/townhome developer:**

- **Octapharma is now a closed deal.** Rock Hill City Council's unanimous 7-0 vote removes the execution-risk discount that held this deal at 87 last cycle. Model Rock Hill/Fort Mill absorption with meaningfully higher confidence — the next milestone to watch is a groundbreaking date (construction is expected to start 2027).
- **Two major reversals hit the region this cycle, both worth flagging even though neither is a ranked deal.** SC Ports Authority's pause of **all** container operations at the Leatherman Terminal (North Charleston) took effect as scheduled on **2026-08-01** — today — with no reopening timeline given; Phase 2 wharf construction continues, but 400 direct + ~1,200 indirect jobs are now in limbo. Separately, **Chatham County terminated its VinFast EV-megafactory incentive agreement on 2026-07-28** after the company's job target collapsed from 7,500 to ~1,400 and it failed to meet even that reduced threshold — a multi-year Triangle-adjacent megaproject story now formally dead, alongside NC's Attorney General's pending suit to force VinFast to sell back the land.
- **Aspida Financial Services (Durham) is the strongest near-miss on the board.** 1,000 jobs at a stated $137,288 average wage and $28M investment would clear every ranked threshold — but the announcement itself is dated 2025-11-19, before this window opens, and no in-window milestone (lease signing, groundbreaking, hiring event) was found this cycle. This is the highest-priority item to recheck next cycle; if any forward-moving milestone lands inside the window, it should promote directly to Ranked.
- **Scout Motors' hiring signal remains strong even as the delivery timeline keeps slipping.** The July 29 hiring event for 70+ maintenance-technician roles went off as scheduled — near-term housing demand in northern Richland/Fairfield stays intact — but "early 2028" is now the plainly stated customer-delivery target, one more concrete data point in a now-multi-cycle pattern of timeline slippage.
- **JetZero's EXIM financing Letter of Interest is a durability signal, not a hiring signal.** Up to $3B in potential federal financing strengthens the case that Greensboro's $4.7B megaproject reaches full build-out, but it does not change the state's own JDIG-amendment timeline (hiring ramp begins 2028-2029, full 14,500-job target due 2037).
- **Watch-tier housekeeping this cycle:** two long-stale items were removed (Snider Fleet Solutions — three years of no news; FN America — three consecutive quiet cycles), while roughly ten new items were added, most notably Lenovo's Whitsett campus (capital investment nearly doubled to $145M) and a flagged scope-reduction at EnerSys's Greenville lithium-cell project (capacity cut ~75-80%, timeline slipped to ~2031).

## 3. Market Writeups

### 1. Charlotte / Uptown — SMBC Group (Mecklenburg, NC) — Score 93
**What happened:** No new in-window milestone found this cycle. The bank's lease of the former One Wells Fargo Center and active hiring for 70+ roles (reported 2026-05-04) remain the most recent confirmed developments.
**Why it matters:** A quiet cycle for a deal this well-documented is not a red flag.
**Housing-demand implication:** Unchanged — strong, urban-concentrated Class-A multifamily and luxury for-sale townhome demand in Uptown/South End/Dilworth, with first worker arrivals still tracking to fall 2026.

### 2. Rock Hill / York County — Octapharma "Project Palmetto Rock" (York, SC) — Score 90 — Gaining Momentum
**What happened:** Rock Hill City Council voted **unanimously (7-0) on 2026-07-23** at a special meeting to approve both (a) the sale of 50 acres in Palmetto Research Park (the former Panthers practice site) to Octapharma, and (b) the amended tax-incentive agreement carried over from York County's 3rd-reading vote (2026-07-08, 4-3). This resolves the prior cycle's single largest open question — whether Rock Hill would ratify the amended tax-share terms — decisively in favor of the project. Council members publicly criticized York County over the underlying tax-split fight during the same meeting, but the vote itself to proceed was unanimous. No groundbreaking date has been announced yet; construction is expected to start in 2027.
**Why it matters:** The deal is now fully cleared on both the county and city side — a materially lower-risk position than the "genuinely unresolved" status carried into this report last cycle.
**Housing-demand implication:** Unchanged in scale (dual-tier: $141k HQ roles support luxury for-sale/Class-A; $102k manufacturing roles support premium workforce-plus rental/townhome product in Rock Hill/Fort Mill), now supported by materially higher deal-certainty.
**Source:** [WBTV, 2026-07-23](https://www.wbtv.com/2026/07/23/rock-hills-octapharma-projects-moved-forward-what-we-know/) · [Fort Mill Sun, 2026-07-23](https://www.fortmillsun.com/2026/07/23/rock-hill-council-clears-octapharma-deal-in-7-0-votes-as-members-blast-york-county-over-incentive-fight/)

### 3. Blythewood / North Columbia — Scout Motors (Richland, SC) — Score 85 — Updated

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (new deals score low; repeated/gaining-momentum deals score higher) |
