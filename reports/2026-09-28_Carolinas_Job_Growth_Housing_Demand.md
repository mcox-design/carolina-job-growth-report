### Week of 2026-09-28 (Week 11)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-03-28 → 2026-09-28** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note — read first.** This is Week 11, built on the 2026-09-21 report. All five regional research passes ran this cycle (Charlotte metro, NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry) via dedicated parallel research agents, each re-verifying every region-specific Ranked/Watch entry and searching broadly for new qualifying announcements. **WebFetch (direct page retrieval) again returned blocked/egress-restricted for the large majority of news/government/company/SEC domains attempted this cycle**, regardless of agent; nearly every finding below relies on WebSearch-result content and snippets, which do surface real, retrievable source URLs, cross-checked across multiple independent queries per claim. No numbers were invented anywhere in this report; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.
>
> **Window note:** the trailing 6-month window advanced exactly one week (2026-03-21→09-21 to **2026-03-28→09-28**). No Ranked entry's sole milestone aged out of window this cycle — Citigroup's April 23, 2026 anchor and Scout Motors HQ's March 28, 2026 filing date both remain comfortably inside the new window.

|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |

| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction, or already open) |
