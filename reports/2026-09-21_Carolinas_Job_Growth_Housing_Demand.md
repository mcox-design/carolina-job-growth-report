### Week of 2026-09-21 (Week 10)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-03-21 → 2026-09-21** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note — read first.** This is Week 10, built on the 2026-09-14 report. All five regional research passes ran this cycle (Charlotte metro, NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry) via dedicated parallel research agents, each re-verifying every region-specific Ranked/Watch entry and searching broadly for new qualifying announcements. **WebFetch (direct page retrieval) again returned blocked/egress-restricted for the large majority of news/government/company domains attempted this cycle**, regardless of agent; nearly every finding below relies on WebSearch-result content and snippets, which do surface real, retrievable source URLs, cross-checked across multiple independent queries per claim. No numbers were invented anywhere in this report; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.
>
> **Window note:** the trailing 6-month window advanced exactly one week (2026-03-14→09-14 to **2026-03-21→09-21**). This shift forced a genuine reckoning with several long-tracked entries whose only-ever-confirmed dated milestones have now aged fully out of window: **Aspida Financial Services was removed from Ranked**, and **Ralliant Corp., Jewelers Mutual Group, and Wolfspeed were removed from Markets to Watch** (Wolfspeed's WARN-notice dating ambiguity — unresolved for two prior cycles — was conclusively resolved this cycle as a mid-2025 event, over a year outside the window). **Citigroup** was at the same risk but survived: a dedicated recheck found a genuinely dated, in-window hiring-progress update (2026-04-23) that keeps it Ranked. **Environmental Air Systems** was also removed from Watch (four consecutive quiet cycles), and **Google's Berkeley/Dorchester, SC data-center entry** was downgraded to stale background after eight consecutive cycles with no dated 2026 update.

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction, or already open) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (new deals score low; repeated/gaining-momentum deals score higher) |

**Score sensitivity to data quality:** SMBC, AbbVie, and JetZero all moved this cycle purely on capital-certainty/momentum grounds (recruiting/move-in firmness, a new incentive layer, and a finalized funding/delay resolution respectively) rather than any change to job count or wage — a reminder that this scoring model rewards de-risking events, not just headline growth.
