# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-07-13 (Week 3)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-01-13 → 2026-07-13** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle/Triad, Upstate SC, Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note (read first).** This is Week 3, built on the 2026-07-06 report. Five parallel research passes covered Charlotte metro, the NC Triangle, the NC Triad/WNC/Coast, Upstate SC, and SC Midlands/Lowcountry, each explicitly re-checking every prior-week ranked and watch-tier entry for in-window updates before searching for new candidates. **No brand-new qualifying (500+ jobs / $100k+ wage) deal was found in any of the five sub-regions this cycle** — all 8 ranked entries from the 2026-07-06 report carry forward, several with real updates. Two near-miss candidates that cross the job-count and wage thresholds on paper (Maersk, Citigroup) were nonetheless excluded, for consistency with this dataset's established rule that a milestone must represent genuine forward movement (groundbreaking, hiring ramp, investment increase) — a hiring delay or a grand-opening tied to a pre-window commitment does not qualify. No numbers were invented; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.

**Top markets this week (by composite score):**

1. **Charlotte / Uptown (Mecklenburg, NC) — SMBC Group, Score 93 (unchanged).** No new in-window milestone this cycle; the 2,000-job, $165,316-avg-wage lease/hiring plan remains on track for first workers arriving fall 2026.

4. **Durham / RTP (Durham, NC) — AbbVie, Score 84 (unchanged).** No new milestone this cycle; the $1.4B/734-job campus remains on track.
5. **Charlotte (Mecklenburg, NC) — Capital Group, Score 81 (Updated, +2).** The previously Estimated/Inferred ~$190k wage figure is now confirmed as a **stated $194,141 average** in the NC Commerce JDIG record — removing the prior wage-confidence discount.
6. **Holly Springs / SW Wake (NC) — Genentech, Score 80 (unchanged).** No new milestone this cycle.
7. **Greensboro / PTI Airport (Guilford, NC) — JetZero, Score 79 (Updated, mixed, -2).** Groundbreaking was actually held on 2026-06-15 — real, physical construction progress — but the state's own JDIG amendment confirms the hiring ramp has slipped even further: zero jobs now projected for 2027, and the full 14,500-job target is now due by 2037 (pushed a further year from 2036).
8. **Durham / Morrisville (Durham & Wake, NC) — Novartis, Score 77 (unchanged).** No new milestone this cycle.

**Key takeaways for a MF/townhome developer:**

- **This is the first cycle with zero new qualifying deals across all five sub-regions.** That is not the same as "nothing happening" — six of eight ranked deals had genuine updates — but the pipeline of brand-new 500+/$100k+ Carolinas megaprojects has gone quiet for the moment. Two candidates that clear the raw thresholds (Maersk at $100,962 stated wage; Citigroup at $131,832 stated wage) were excluded because their in-window "milestone" was a hiring delay and a grand-opening of a pre-window commitment, respectively — worth re-checking if either produces a genuine forward-moving event.
- **Octapharma remains the region's most consequential open story.** County-level approval keeps advancing, but on a narrower margin, and the amended deal (stripping most of Rock Hill's own tax share) still needs a city council vote that had not happened as of 2026-07-13. Model Rock Hill/Fort Mill absorption with continued — not resolved — execution risk.
- **JetZero is a genuine "construction real, timeline slower" story.** A groundbreaking ceremony is tangible evidence the project is happening, but the state's JDIG amendment is the clearest confirmation yet that hiring-driven housing demand in Guilford County should be modeled against a 2028-2029 (or later) ramp, not anything sooner.
- **South Carolina produced two notable downside signals this cycle.** SC Ports Authority will pause **all** operations at the Leatherman Terminal in North Charleston starting August 1, 2026 (high labor costs, weak container volume) — a reversal on a previously tracked Watch-tier item. Separately, TigerDC's $3.0 billion "Project Spero" AI data center in Spartanburg County was **formally withdrawn** after the county council voted down its tax incentive.
- **Boom Supersonic's contractual deadline is now six months out with no positive movement.** The Greensboro hangar remains largely idle as of the most recent reporting, and the company's own CEO has said production is roughly two years away — do not model any near-term Guilford County demand from this project, and watch closely whether Boom clears (or misses) the Dec. 31, 2026 500-employee floor.
- **Capital Group's wage upgrade is a small but real data-quality win.** Moving from an inferred payroll-math estimate to a stated NC Commerce figure ($194,141) is exactly the kind of confirmation this report exists to track, and it moved Capital Group up two ranks this cycle.

## 3. Market Writeups

### 1. Charlotte / Uptown — SMBC Group (Mecklenburg, NC) — Score 93
**What happened:** No new in-window milestone found this cycle. The bank's lease of the former One Wells Fargo Center and active hiring for 70+ roles (reported 2026-05-04) remain the most recent confirmed developments.
**Why it matters:** A quiet cycle after two consecutive weeks of forward motion (announcement → lease/hiring) is not a red flag for a deal this well-documented.
**Housing-demand implication:** Unchanged — strong, urban-concentrated Class-A multifamily and luxury for-sale townhome demand in Uptown/South End/Dilworth, with first worker arrivals still tracking to fall 2026.

### 2. Rock Hill / York County — Octapharma "Project Palmetto Rock" (York, SC) — Score 87 — Updated (mixed)
**What happened:** York County Council approved **third and final reading** of the fee-in-lieu-of-tax agreement on **2026-07-08** by a **4-3 vote** — narrower than the 2nd reading's 5-2 margin. Under the amended terms, roughly 85% of Rock Hill's own expected property-tax share is redirected, mostly to Rock Hill Schools. Rock Hill's Planning Commission separately recommended rezoning approval the same day. Critically, **Rock Hill City Council has not yet voted** on the revised tax-share structure as of 2026-07-13 — the city's earlier conditional consent (from before the amendment) is void under the new terms, leaving the deal's final shape genuinely open.
**Why it matters:** The deal keeps clearing procedural hurdles, but a tightening vote margin and an outstanding city-level vote are real, not cosmetic, execution risks — this is not yet a closed deal.
**Housing-demand implication:** Unchanged in scale (dual-tier: $141k HQ roles support luxury for-sale/Class-A; $102k manufacturing roles support premium workforce-plus rental/townhome product in Rock Hill/Fort Mill), but timing should still be modeled with meaningful uncertainty until Rock Hill City Council votes.
**Source:** [WBTV, 2026-07-10](https://www.wbtv.com/2026/07/10/york-county-council-approves-project-palmetto-rock-deal-rock-hill-city-council-vote-deal/) · [Fort Mill Sun, 2026-07-09](https://www.fortmillsun.com/2026/07/09/york-county-steers-octapharma-tax-revenue-to-rock-hill-schools-in-4-3-vote/) · [Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/york-county/news/york-county-rock-hill-tax-incentive-octapharma-plasma/article_f620e54d-064b-4bfe-be5e-9cc553593743.html) · [Fort Mill Sun, 2026-07-08 (rezoning)](https://www.fortmillsun.com/2026/07/08/rock-hill-planning-commission-recommends-rezoning-for-octapharma-manufacturing-plant/)

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (new deals score low; repeated/gaining-momentum deals score higher) |

**Score sensitivity to data quality:** inferred wages cap the "salary" factor below a stated equivalent (Capital Group's upgrade from inferred to stated wage moved its score up); timeline/execution risk (Octapharma's narrower vote margin and outstanding city-council vote, JetZero's further-extended hiring ramp) discounts "project certainty."
