# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-07-06 (Week 2)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-01-06 → 2026-07-06** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle/Triad, Upstate SC, Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note (read first).** This is Week 2, built on the 2026-06-24 baseline report. Five parallel research passes covered Charlotte metro, the NC Triangle, the NC Triad/WNC/Coast, Upstate SC, and SC Midlands/Lowcountry. Several primary state press-release domains (commerce.nc.gov, governor.nc.gov, governor.sc.gov, sccommerce.com, wake.gov) returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch this cycle; where that happened, facts below are corroborated against **2+ independent secondary outlets** (WRAL, Axios, Post and Courier, Business NC, local TV, county EDCs) rather than a single unfetched primary link, and confidence is marked accordingly. Two claims found only on non-authoritative aggregator/relocation blogs (a "Barclays 1,500 jobs" claim and an "Energizer HQ relocation" claim, both Wake County) could not be corroborated anywhere else and are excluded rather than reported as fact. No numbers were invented; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.

## 1. Executive Summary

**Top markets this week (by composite score):**

1. **Charlotte / Uptown (Mecklenburg, NC) — SMBC Group, Score 93.** Momentum is building on the region's top story: SMBC leased the 42-story former One Wells Fargo Center in Uptown and began hiring for 70+ roles, with first workers expected fall 2026.
2. **Rock Hill / York County, SC — Octapharma "Project Palmetto Rock," Score 88 (NEW).** The single largest new signal this cycle — a $1.5B plasma-biopharma HQ + manufacturing campus at the former Carolina Panthers HQ site, with **1,200 new + 300 relocated jobs (1,500+ total)** and **two stated wage tiers both above $100k** ($141,502 HQ avg / $102,752 manufacturing avg). York County Council gave second-reading approval 2026-06-29, though a late tax-share amendment has drawn local controversy.
3. **Blythewood / N. Columbia (Richland, SC) — Scout Motors, Score 86.** The EV plant crossed a real production milestone in June 2026: first welded vehicle body completed, first production associates on the line, first parts received.
4. **Durham / RTP (Durham, NC) — AbbVie, Score 84.** No new milestone this cycle; the $1.4B/734-job campus remains on track per the April 2026 announcement.
5. **Greensboro / PTI Airport (Guilford, NC) — JetZero, Score 81.** Mixed signal: the state allocated **$133.9M** (2026-06-30) to unstick site infrastructure, but the company's own hiring ramp slipped a full year (zero 2027 hires now projected; ramp shifts to 2028–2029) due to the state budget delay that held up construction.
6. **Holly Springs / SW Wake (NC) — Genentech, Score 80.** No new milestone this cycle; investment-doubling story from January stands.
7. **Charlotte (Mecklenburg, NC) — Capital Group, Score 79.** Signed a 196,940 sq ft lease at One Independence Center (Uptown), confirming the operations hub is moving from "announced" to "occupying real estate."
8. **Durham / Morrisville (Durham & Wake, NC) — Novartis, Score 77 (NEW to this report).** Excluded at baseline as pre-window (original Nov. 2025 deal), Novartis now qualifies via a genuine **in-window investment-increase milestone**: an additional $220M announced 2026-04-30 for a new API manufacturing building, part of the same 700-job/$111,161-avg-wage package (total investment now ~$991M).

**Key takeaways for a MF/townhome developer:**

- **Charlotte-region SC (York County) just produced the strongest housing-demand signal of either report cycle.** Octapharma's dual six-figure wage tiers (HQ *and* manufacturing both stated above $100k) is a first in this dataset — SC deals are usually wage-opaque. This directly supports both luxury Class-A and premium workforce-plus product in Rock Hill/Fort Mill, a submarket the report had under-covered.
- **Four of six baseline-ranked deals are showing forward motion, not just survival.** SMBC, Scout Motors, and Capital Group all cleared real occupancy/production milestones this cycle (leases signed, first vehicle built) — a good early signal that April's paper commitments are becoming physical ones.
- **JetZero is a genuine mixed signal.** The $133.9M state infrastructure allocation is a vote of confidence, but the company's own hiring deadline slipped a full year — model Guilford County absorption on a 2028–2029 timeline, not 2027–2028.
- **A second PTI Airport aerospace project (Boom Supersonic) is now a downside risk, not a tailwind.** Its Greensboro hangar reportedly sits largely idle against a Dec. 31, 2026 contractual deadline to reach 500 employees or risk lease termination — do not stack this on top of JetZero's job count in any Guilford County housing model.
- **The Triangle's in-window activity this cycle is almost entirely milestone-driven, not new deals** — Novartis's investment increase is the one exception that newly qualifies. A large but wage-ambiguous event to watch closely: the proposed WakeMed/Atrium Health merger (3,300 jobs statewide, $2B in Wake County), still under review as of June 2026.
- **NC's stated-wage discipline continues to make it easier to underwrite than SC** — though Octapharma just became the first SC deal in this dataset to publish two clean six-figure averages, worth watching as a possible shift in SC disclosure norms.

## 3. Market Writeups

### 1. Charlotte / Uptown — SMBC Group (Mecklenburg, NC) — Score 93
**What happened:** SMBC leased the 42-story former One Wells Fargo Center in Uptown Charlotte and is actively hiring for 70+ roles as of early May 2026, with first workers expected to report as early as fall 2026 — real estate and hiring now following the April 2026 announcement of 2,000 jobs at a stated $165,316 average salary.
**Why it matters:** A landmark Uptown tower changing hands to a Japanese bank moving from announcement to occupancy is the clearest sign yet that this is executing, not just a press release.
**Housing-demand implication:** Unchanged from baseline — strong, urban-concentrated Class-A multifamily and luxury for-sale townhome demand in Uptown/South End/Dilworth, now with a more concrete hiring timeline (fall 2026 first arrivals).
**Source:** [Axios Charlotte, 2026-05-04](https://www.axios.com/local/charlotte/2026/05/04/smbc-headquarters-wells-fargo-center-uptown)

### 2. Rock Hill / York County — Octapharma "Project Palmetto Rock" (York, SC) — Score 88 — NEW
**What happened:** Octapharma, the world's largest privately-owned plasma fractionator, is building a **$1.5 billion** headquarters + manufacturing campus at the former Carolina Panthers HQ site (Palmetto Research Park, off I-77 Exit 81). The deal brings **1,200 new jobs plus 300 relocated from an existing Charlotte-area Octapharma Plasma facility** (1,500+ total). County-presented figures show **HQ positions averaging $141,502/year and manufacturing positions averaging $102,752/year** — both above the $100k bar, and both *stated*, which is unusual for an SC deal. York County Council approved second reading of the fee-in-lieu-of-tax agreement on 2026-06-29 by a 5-2 vote, but an amendment stripped the City of Rock Hill's share of the tax break and cut Rock Hill Schools' share to 31.9%, prompting at least one outlet to frame the deal as "in jeopardy."
**Why it matters:** This is the first SC deal in this dataset to publish two clean six-figure wage tiers, and the largest new-jobs commitment found in the Charlotte-metro SC counties (York/Lancaster/Chester) since tracking began.
**Housing-demand implication:** Strong and dual-tier — $141k HQ/executive roles support luxury for-sale and Class-A multifamily in Rock Hill/Fort Mill; $102k manufacturing roles support premium workforce-plus rental and townhome product in the same submarket. The tax-share controversy is a real execution risk worth monitoring, not a reason to discount the jobs/wage figures themselves.
**Source:** [Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/york-county/news/rock-hill-plasma-panthers-octapharma-billion/article_fbd570b1-6451-406b-bb8c-92a53fad0d7c.html) · [Fort Mill Sun, 2026-06-29](https://www.fortmillsun.com/2026/06/29/york-county-council-passes-second-reading-of-1-5-billion-biopharma-deal-data-center-moratorium/) · [WRHI](https://www.wrhi.com/2026/07/octapharma-deal-in-jeopardy-after-york-county-amendment-strips-rock-hills-tax-share-213896) · [WSOC-TV](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/york-county-council-approves-tax-break-amendment-octapharmas-us-headquarters/N2PPD3KD4JGHHPNO4B7KZWORVQ/) · [QC News](https://www.qcnews.com/news/u-s/york-county/biopharmaceutical-company-revealed-that-could-create-1200-jobs-in-rock-hill/)

### 3. Blythewood / North Columbia — Scout Motors (Richland, SC) — Score 86
**What happened:** Scout Motors cleared a real production milestone in its June 2026 company update: the first welded Traveler vehicle body was completed in the Body Shop — the plant's first-ever vehicle-body weld — alongside the first production associates hired, first parts received, and factory power-on. This follows the April 20, 2026 grand opening of the $25M readySC training center (600+ hired at that point).
**Why it matters:** Moving from "hiring" to "welding the first body" is a meaningfully different (and lower-risk) stage of execution than the training-center milestone alone.
**Housing-demand implication:** Unchanged from baseline — bifurcated demand (salaried/professional staff support higher-income product; the large hourly base drives workforce/missing-middle rental) in northern Richland/Fairfield, now backed by tangible production evidence.
**Source:** [Scout Motors company blog, June 2026](https://blog.scoutmotors.com/june-2026-scout-motors-production-center-update/)

### 4. Durham / RTP — AbbVie (Durham, NC) — Score 84
**What happened:** No new in-window milestone was found this cycle beyond the April 22, 2026 announcement (734 jobs at a stated $118,041 average, $1.4B campus).
**Why it matters:** A quiet cycle for a deal already this well-documented is not a red flag — construction/hiring timelines run through 2028–2031 per the original release.
**Housing-demand implication:** Unchanged — strong high-end MF and move-up for-sale demand around Durham/RTP, spillover into Wake/Orange.
**Source:** [AbbVie press release, 2026-04-22](https://news.abbvie.com/2026-04-22-AbbVie-Selects-North-Carolina-for-New-1-4-Billion-Manufacturing-Campus)

### 5. Greensboro / PTI Airport — JetZero (Guilford, NC) — Score 81 — Updated (mixed signal)
**What happened:** Two competing developments landed in-window. On the positive side, the NC state budget (finalized 2026-06-30, after months without an approved FY budget) allocated **$133.9 million** for JetZero site infrastructure — the funding shortfall that had stalled construction start. On the negative side, JetZero itself requested and received a **one-year hiring-deadline extension**: the original Dec. 31, 2026 hiring target moves to Dec. 31, 2027, with the company now projecting essentially zero 2027 hires and the real ramp shifting to 2028–2029.
**Why it matters:** The project's ultimate scale (14,500 jobs, $4.7B) is unchanged and now better-funded, but the near-term timeline that housing developers would model against has slipped a full year.
**Housing-demand implication:** Same long-run scale as baseline, but push absorption modeling out roughly 12 months — peak demand pressure now more plausibly lands 2028–2029 rather than 2027–2028.
**Source:** [Carolina Journal — hiring delay](https://www.carolinajournal.com/jetzero-delays-hiring-timeline-amid-budget-holdup/) · [Carolina Journal — $133.9M allocation, 2026-06-30](https://www.carolinajournal.com/state-budget-allocates-133-9m-for-delayed-jetzero-project/) · [WRAL, May 2026](https://www.wral.com/news/nccapitol/jetzero-adjusts-north-carolina-hiring-targets-commerce-incentives-may-2026/) · [Greensboro News & Record](https://greensboro.com/news/local/business/development/article_a76e0066-6373-494a-aa7e-3bd7c3872bf9.html)

### 8. Durham / Morrisville — Novartis (Durham & Wake, NC) — Score 77 — New to this report (in-window re-qualification)
**What happened:** Novartis's original Nov. 19, 2025 deal (700 jobs at a stated $118,041→ correction: **$111,161** average salary, originally $771M investment) was excluded from the June 24 baseline report as pre-window. On **2026-04-30**, Novartis announced an **additional $220 million investment** in a new 56,200 sq ft API (active pharmaceutical ingredient) manufacturing building at Pathway Triangle in Morrisville — part of the same 700-job package, bringing Novartis to five facilities across three NC sites and total investment to roughly **$991 million**. Wake County's own release cites &gt;$231M invested and 100+ high-paying jobs specifically in Morrisville.
**Why it matters:** Per this report's rules, a material milestone (here, an investment increase) landing inside the trailing 6-month window brings a previously out-of-scope deal back into scope — the same logic that put Genentech in the baseline report. Facility opening targeted 2027–2028.
**Housing-demand implication:** Reinforces sustained high-wage biomanufacturing hiring in the Wake/Durham corridor (stated $111,161 average vs. Durham County's $102,817 and Wake's $76,643), supporting continued upper-income housing absorption around Morrisville/RTP.
**Source:** [WRAL, 2026-04-30](https://www.wral.com/news/local/novartis-pharmaceuticals-expands-nc-presence-morrisville-plant-april-2026/) · [Wake County Government](https://www.wake.gov/news/novartis-pharmaceuticals-announces-220-m-expansion-morrisville)

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (new deals score low; repeated/gaining-momentum deals score higher) |

**Score sensitivity to data quality:** inferred wages cap the "salary" factor below a stated equivalent; timeline/execution risk (e.g., JetZero's hiring delay, Octapharma's tax-share controversy) discounts "project certainty."
