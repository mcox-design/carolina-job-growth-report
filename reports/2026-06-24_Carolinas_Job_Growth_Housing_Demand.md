# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-06-24 (Inaugural / Baseline Report — Week 1)

**Coverage window:** Announcements dated **2025-12-24 → 2026-06-24** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle/Triad, Upstate SC, Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note (read first).** The window is set to a trailing **6 months** (back to 2025-12-24). This pulls **Genentech** (Holly Springs, Jan 20) and **Siemens Energy** (Feb 4) into scope versus a 90-day view. Note that the large **November 2025 cluster** — Novartis & Aspida (Durham), Vulcan Elements (Johnston), Maersk & Pacific Life (Charlotte) — is **still outside** the window (>6 months back) and remains under **Excluded/Noise** with real dates; widen to ~7+ months to capture it. Every figure below was verified against a primary or near-primary source (NC Commerce / NC Governor / EDPNC / SC Commerce / SC Governor releases, company press releases, or major business journals). Where a number was not published, it is written **"Not disclosed."** Inferred salaries are explicitly labeled **"Estimated/Inferred"** with their basis. No numbers were invented.

**Top markets this week (by composite score):**

Reinforced by Capital Group's 600-job operations hub (implied ~$190k payroll-per-head). Charlotte's finance-tech core is the clearest Class-A multifamily / urban-townhome demand driver in the Carolinas right now.

3. **Durham / RTP (Durham, NC) — Score 84.** AbbVie's $1.4B greenfield campus = **734 permanent jobs at $118,041 average** + 2,000+ construction jobs. Premium life-sciences demand for Durham and spillover Wake/Orange.
4. **Greensboro / PTI Airport (Guilford, NC) — Score 82.** JetZero's **14,500-job, $4.7B** aerospace plant physically **broke ground 2026-06-15** — the largest job commitment in NC history now showing real movement. Wage ~$89k (below the $100k bar but well above market); watch a reported state-budget hiring-timeline delay.
5. **Holly Springs / SW Wake (NC) — Genentech, Score 80.** Jan 2026 investment doubling to **~$2B** pushes the biomanufacturing site over **500 jobs** at a stated **~$120k** average — newly in scope under the 6-month window.
6. **Charlotte (Mecklenburg, NC) — Capital Group, Score 78.** 600 high-skill finance/tech jobs, $60M, implied ~$190k payroll-per-head (Estimated/Inferred).

**Key takeaways for a MF/townhome developer:**

- **Charlotte is the standout high-income story.** Two finance/finance-tech operations hubs (SMBC, Capital Group) landed inside the window, both with six-figure average pay and both Uptown/urban-core oriented → premier Class-A multifamily and urban for-sale townhome demand in Uptown / South End / Dilworth / Ballantyne.
- **The Triangle is the #2 cluster on life sciences.** AbbVie ($118k) and Genentech (~$120k) are both high-wage biomanufacturing anchors — Durham and SW Wake (Holly Springs/Apex/Fuquay) carry the strongest non-Charlotte high-income housing signal.
- **NC's stated-wage discipline makes it easy to underwrite; SC's wage opacity does not.** Every qualifying NC deal (SMBC, AbbVie, Capital Group, JetZero) carries a *stated* average wage in its incentive release. SC deals (Scout, Ferrara, Suniva, USA Rare Earth) largely do **not** disclose wages — so SC income tiers lean on inferred/role-based estimates and are flagged accordingly.
- **The biggest SC headcount stories are workforce-tier, not high-income.** Ferrara (1,000 jobs, Orangeburg) and Suniva (564, Laurens) clear the 500-job bar but read as **workforce/missing-middle** demand, not luxury — they sit in Markets to Watch, not the high-income ranked table.
- **Manufacturing momentum is broad but sub-threshold in WNC and Upstate SC.** A cluster of 200–490-job advanced-manufacturing wins (Eaton, BorgWarner, USA Rare Earth, FN America, Siemens) is real cumulative demand worth tracking even though no single deal qualifies.

## 3. Market Writeups

### 1. Charlotte / Uptown — SMBC Group (Mecklenburg, NC) — Score 92
**What happened:** Japan's SMBC Group (Sumitomo Mitsui) selected Charlotte for a second U.S. headquarters / bank-operations expansion — **2,000 jobs over six years at a stated $165,316 average salary** (vs. the $90,706 Mecklenburg County average), $50.5M investment, backed by a JDIG worth up to $70.0M over 12 years.
**Why it matters:** This is the highest-wage, largest white-collar commitment in the window, and it deepens Charlotte's standing as the #2 U.S. banking center. Average pay at ~1.8× the county average is exactly the income profile that fills Class-A units and high-end for-sale townhomes.
**Housing-demand implication:** Strong, urban-concentrated. Expect absorption pressure on Uptown / South End / Dilworth luxury multifamily first (relocating professionals rent before buying), then move-up townhome demand in inner suburbs. A top-priority submarket for new MF/townhome pipeline.
**Source:** [NC Commerce, 2026-04-07](https://www.commerce.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2026/04/07/governor-stein-announces-2000-new-jobs-japans-smbc-group-selects-charlotte-bank-operations-expansion) · [Governor's Office](https://governor.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2026/04/07/governor-stein-announces-2000-new-jobs-japans-smbc-group-selects-charlotte-bank-operations-expansion)

### 2. Blythewood / North Columbia — Scout Motors (Richland, SC) — Score 85
**What happened:** Scout Motors' $2B EV truck/SUV plant moved from announced project to **active hiring** — 600+ workers already hired toward 4,000 at full capacity, a $25M readySC training center opened (grand opening 2026-04-20), and production is targeted to begin by end of 2026. Line wages reported at $30–$37.50/hr; salaried engineering/quality/logistics roles materially higher.
**Why it matters:** This is the dominant employment engine of the I-77 corridor north of Columbia and is now demonstrably *moving* (the qualifying basis is the in-window hiring/training milestone, not the 2023 selection). A supplier ecosystem will compound the headcount.
**Housing-demand implication:** Strong and bifurcated — salaried/professional staff support higher-income for-sale and Class-A rental in Blythewood / NE Columbia; the large hourly base drives workforce/missing-middle rental. Both product types underwriteable in northern Richland/Fairfield.
**Source:** [WIS, 2026-04-20 (training center / 600+ hired)](https://www.wistv.com/2026/04/20/scout-motors-hold-grand-opening-blythewood-area-training-center/) · [WLTX (plant/wages)](https://www.wltx.com/article/news/local/midlands/building-the-midlands/scout-motors-plant-blythewood-update/101-c64d17ed-4b85-4fae-b866-e7e1a08021f1)

### 3. Durham / RTP — AbbVie (Durham, NC) — Score 84
**What happened:** AbbVie selected Durham for a new **$1.4B, 185-acre** pharmaceutical manufacturing + lab campus — **734 permanent jobs at a stated $118,041 average** (vs. $102,817 county average) plus 2,000+ construction jobs. JDIG up to ~$19.3M over 12 years + city/county incentives. Construction starts 2026; completion ~end of 2028; hiring through ~2031.
**Why it matters:** Confirms the Triangle's life-sciences manufacturing cluster is still expanding and adds a marquee, high-wage anchor. With AbbVie, NC hosts 8 of the 10 largest pharma companies by revenue.
**Housing-demand implication:** Strong. 734 jobs at ~$118k support high-end multifamily and move-up for-sale around Durham/RTP, with spillover into Wake/Orange. Construction-phase rental demand near-term, permanent high-income demand 2028+.
**Source:** [AbbVie press release, 2026-04-22](https://news.abbvie.com/2026-04-22-AbbVie-Selects-North-Carolina-for-New-1-4-Billion-Manufacturing-Campus) · [WRAL (salary + incentives)](https://www.wral.com/news/nccapitol/abbvie-730-jobs-state-officials-north-carolina-incentives-april-2026/)

### 4. Greensboro / PTI Airport — JetZero (Guilford, NC) — Score 82
**What happened:** JetZero's **14,500-job, $4.7B** blended-wing-body aircraft plant **physically broke ground 2026-06-15** — the largest job commitment in NC history. Stated average wage ~$89,340 (from the June 2025 selection; not restated at groundbreaking). State JDIG up to ~$1.02B over 37 years.
**Why it matters:** The in-window event is real movement (site work starting) on a transformational project. Even at ~$89k (below the $100k bar), 14,500 jobs is broad, sustained, above-market demand across Greensboro and into the Randolph/Alamance commuter shed.
**Caveat:** Carolina Journal reported a hiring-timeline delay tied to a state budget holdup — **monitor execution risk.** Treat the wage as the 2025-stated figure.
**Housing-demand implication:** Potentially transformational long-term — multi-year construction demand then 14,500 permanent jobs. Supports a wide band of rental and for-sale product across Guilford and adjacent counties. Largest *volume* signal in the report; timing risk is the discount.
**Source:** [NC Commerce, 2026-06-15 (groundbreaking)](https://www.commerce.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2026/06/15/governor-stein-celebrates-jetzero-groundbreaking-launch-greensboro-airplane-makers-14500-job-project)

### 5. Holly Springs / SW Wake — Genentech (Wake, NC) — Score 80
**What happened:** On 2026-01-20 Genentech announced it would **more than double** its Holly Springs investment to **~$2 billion**, expanding the biomanufacturing facility. The expansion adds ~100 manufacturing jobs (bringing the site over **500 total**) plus 1,500+ construction jobs; facility operational by 2029.
**Salary basis:** The original 2025 tranche carried a **stated $119,833 average** (≈$120k, ~56% above the Wake County average) — reaffirmed in 2026 coverage. No new incentive on the +100; the original JDIG is up to ~$9.85M over 12 years.
**Why it matters:** This is a 6-month-window addition (a 90-day view missed it). It deepens the Triangle's life-sciences manufacturing base in fast-growing southwest Wake and reinforces AbbVie's signal across the metro.
**Housing-demand implication:** Strong higher-income for-sale and Class-A rental demand in SW Wake (Holly Springs / Apex / Fuquay-Varina); ~$120k average is well above county median, with a sizeable near-term construction workforce.
**Caveat:** The in-window *event* is an investment doubling; the net new headcount increment is +100 (site crosses 500 cumulatively). Wage figure traces to the original award, not a fresh 2026 number.
**Source:** [Genentech press release, 2026-01-20](https://www.gene.com/media/press-releases/15096/2026-01-20/genentech-more-than-doubles-investment-i) · [WRAL](https://www.wral.com/business/genentech-expansion-holly-springs-feb-2026/)

### 6. Charlotte — Capital Group (Mecklenburg, NC) — Score 78
**What happened:** Capital Group established a major East Coast operations hub in Charlotte — **600 jobs, $60M**, JDIG up to ~$17.2M over 12 years. Roles cited: software/data/AI engineering, data science, investment operations, global business services.
**Salary basis:** Wage **not officially disclosed**. The release cites an anticipated annual payroll of ~$116M for ~600 jobs, implying **~$190k average** — labeled **Estimated/Inferred** (derived, not stated); roles named are all typically $100k+ in Charlotte.
**Why it matters:** A second six-figure finance/tech operations hub inside the window, reinforcing the same demand pool as SMBC.
**Housing-demand implication:** Strong, urban/inner-suburban. High-skill engineering/data workforce favors walkable South End/Uptown and inner-suburban Class-A and for-sale townhomes.
**Source:** [Governor's Office, 2026-03-26](https://governor.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2026/03/26/governor-stein-announces-capital-group-will-establish-major-operations-hub-charlotte) · [Axios Charlotte](https://charlotte.axios.com/)

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (baseline = low) |

**Score sensitivity to data quality:** inferred wages cap the "salary" factor below a stated equivalent; timeline/execution risk (e.g., JetZero budget delay) discounts "project certainty."
