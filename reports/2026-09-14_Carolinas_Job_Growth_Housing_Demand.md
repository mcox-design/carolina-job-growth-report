# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-09-14 (Week 9)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-03-14 → 2026-09-14** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note — read first.** This is Week 9, built on the 2026-09-07 report. All five regional research passes ran this cycle (Charlotte metro, NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry) via dedicated parallel research agents, each re-verifying every region-specific Ranked/Watch entry and searching broadly for new qualifying announcements. **WebFetch (direct page retrieval) again returned blocked/`EGRESS_BLOCKED` for essentially every news/government domain attempted this cycle**, regardless of agent; every finding below relies on WebSearch-result content and snippets, which do surface real, retrievable source URLs, cross-checked across multiple independent queries per claim. Each region's WebSearch budget (200 calls) was exhausted before every conceivable follow-up query could be run; gaps are flagged explicitly where they affected a finding. No numbers were invented anywhere in this report; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.
>
> **Window note:** the trailing 6-month window advanced exactly one week (2026-03-07→09-07 to **2026-03-14→09-14**). No currently Ranked entry's sole milestone fell out of window this cycle. Ralliant Corp. (2026-03-05) remains flagged "at risk" in the Watch tier. A new dating concern surfaced this cycle for Wolfspeed's tracked WARN notice — multiple sources consistently date it to **June 9, 2025**, not 2026; if correct, its sole Watch-qualifying milestone is now over a year outside the window, and it is flagged "at risk" pending a direct WARN-database check next cycle.

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction, or already open) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (new deals score low; repeated/gaining-momentum deals score higher) |
