**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-04-19 → 2026-10-19** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note — read first.** This is Week 14, built on the 2026-10-12 report. All five regional research passes ran this cycle (Charlotte metro, NC Triangle, NC Triad/WNC/Coastal, Upstate SC, SC Midlands/Lowcountry) via dedicated parallel research agents, each re-verifying region-specific Ranked/Watch entries and searching broadly for new qualifying announcements. **WebFetch (direct page retrieval) again returned blocked/DNS-failure/egress-restricted for the large majority of news/government/company/SEC domains attempted this cycle**, regardless of agent; nearly every finding below relies on WebSearch-result content and snippets, which do surface real, retrievable source URLs, cross-checked across multiple independent queries per claim. No numbers were invented anywhere in this report; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated.
>
> **Window note:** the trailing 6-month window advanced from 2026-04-12→10-12 to **2026-04-19→10-19**. No Ranked entry's sole milestone aged out of window this cycle. One Watch-tier item (Siemens AG power devices, Raleigh/Wendell/Knightdale) — flagged last cycle as sitting just outside the window — was removed after its sole March 2026 milestone aged fully outside the window with no in-window update found.
