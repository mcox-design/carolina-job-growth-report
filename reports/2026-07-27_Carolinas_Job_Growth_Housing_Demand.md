# Carolinas Job Growth & Housing Demand Report
### Week of 2026-07-27 (Week 4)

**Coverage window:** Announcements/material milestones dated **2026-01-27 → 2026-07-27** (trailing 6 months)
**Geography:** North Carolina + South Carolina (Charlotte metro prioritized, then NC Triangle/Triad, Upstate SC, Midlands/Lowcountry)
**Purpose:** Translate high-paying job growth into a forward signal for multifamily (MF) and townhome development demand.

> **Methodology note (read first).** This is Week 4, built on the 2026-07-13 report. Five parallel research passes covered Charlotte metro, the NC Triangle, the NC Triad/WNC/Coast, Upstate SC, and SC Midlands/Lowcountry, each explicitly re-checking every prior-week ranked and watch-tier entry for in-window updates before searching for new candidates. This cycle produced the region's most consequential resolution to date — Rock Hill City Council's unanimous approval of the Octapharma deal — plus the first tier promotion of a previously excluded deal (Citigroup) since Week 2. No numbers were invented; missing figures are marked **"Not disclosed"**; inferred figures are marked **"Estimated/Inferred"** with basis stated. One data-quality correction from the prior cycle is flagged explicitly in §6.

Independently confirmed 2026 hiring progress (Training Center opening, 600+ SC hires, first vehicle body weld) still supports the "Gaining Momentum" tag, but this is a reminder to verify dates on carried-forward facts every cycle.
- **Two strong near-misses added to Watch.** Aspida Financial Services (Durham, 1,000 jobs, a stated $137,288 average wage — comfortably above $100k) clears every Ranked threshold except the window: its only announcement predates 2026-01-27. VinFast (Chatham County) is the opposite story — job count now cut to 1,400 and wage well under $100k, further complicated by North Carolina's attorney general suing to reclaim the site after VinFast missed its construction and hiring deadlines.

### 2. Rock Hill / York County — Octapharma "Project Palmetto Rock" (York, SC) — Score 90 — Updated (+3)
**What happened:** Rock Hill City Council voted **7-0** on **2026-07-23** to approve rezoning, the land sale, and the amended tax-incentive resolution — resolving the outstanding city-level vote flagged as this deal's key risk in the 2026-07-13 report. This follows York County Council's narrower **4-3** third-reading approval on 2026-07-08/09, which restructured the incentive so Rock Hill forgoes ~85% of its own expected project tax revenue (mostly redirected to Rock Hill Schools, an estimated $80M over 40 years) while York County contributes 10% of its own economic-development-fund revenue. Deal scope is unchanged: 1,200 new + 300 relocated jobs (1,500+ total), $1.5B investment, former Carolina Panthers practice site (Palmetto Research Park), ~1/3 of Octapharma's global plasma capacity.
**Why it matters:** This closes out the region's most-watched pending approval. The deal is now fully cleared at both the city and county level, removing the largest single source of execution uncertainty this dataset has tracked.
**Housing-demand implication:** Dual-tier and unchanged in scale ($141k HQ roles support luxury for-sale/Class-A; $102k manufacturing roles support premium workforce-plus rental/townhome product in Rock Hill/Fort Mill), but now modelable with meaningfully less timing uncertainty — construction could start as early as late 2026.
**Source:** [Fort Mill Sun, 2026-07-23](https://www.fortmillsun.com/2026/07/23/rock-hill-council-clears-octapharma-deal-in-7-0-votes-as-members-blast-york-county-over-incentive-fight/) · [Fort Mill Sun, 2026-07-09](https://www.fortmillsun.com/2026/07/09/york-county-steers-octapharma-tax-revenue-to-rock-hill-schools-in-4-3-vote/) · [WSOC-TV](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/after-weeks-debate-rock-hill-approves-octapharma-deal-create-1500-jobs/)

### 3. Blythewood / North Columbia — Scout Motors (Richland, SC) — Score 86 — Gaining Momentum
**What happened:** Scout Motors and readySC opened a **$25M, 32,500-sq-ft Training Center** at the Blythewood site on **2026-04-20**; the company confirmed **600+ hires** at the SC production site, is nearing 100 production-associate/maintenance-technician hires, and expects 200-300 more hires in 2026 (17,000 people have expressed interest via the company's signup page). A **first vehicle body weld** was completed in June 2026 (validation-build phase). A February 2026 report alleged technical integration problems could push customer-facing production to summer 2028; Scout Motors disputes the report and reaffirms 2027 initial production, though its own CEO has told the press customers should expect physical cars "sometime in 2028."
**Correction from the prior report:** The "70+ maintenance-technician pre-hire/interview events beginning 2026-07-29" cited in the 2026-07-13 report traces to **July 2025**, not 2026 (confirmed via Blythewood Online and Scout Motors' own newsroom archive, both dated 2025-07). No 2026 repeat of that specific event was found — this fact has been retired.
**Why it matters:** The near-term housing-demand driver — active hiring — remains strong (600+ confirmed SC hires, training center live, first body weld complete), even as the ultimate production-for-sale timeline continues to carry real risk.
**Housing-demand implication:** Unchanged — bifurcated demand (salaried/professional staff support higher-income product; the large hourly base drives workforce/missing-middle rental) in northern Richland/Fairfield, reinforced by confirmed 2026 hiring activity.
**Source:** [WIStv, 2026-04-20](https://www.wistv.com/2026/04/20/scout-motors-hold-grand-opening-blythewood-area-training-center/) · [Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/columbia/business/scout-motors-training-employee-blythewood-columbia/article_a33afe3e-64f9-4107-bf8b-2380fd9a1465.html) · [Scout Motors company blog, June 2026](https://blog.scoutmotors.com/june-2026-scout-motors-production-center-update/) · [The Drive, Feb. 2026 (delay report)](https://www.thedrive.com/news/vw-group-delays-scout-motors-launch-until-at-least-2028-report)

## 7. Scoring Methodology & Excel-Ready Tables

**Composite score (0–100)** = weighted sum of six factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Job count | 25 | Absolute headcount (scaled; 2,000+ ≈ full, 500 ≈ ~12) |
| Salary / income quality | 25 | Stated avg wage vs. $100k bar; inferred wages discounted |
| Employer & industry strength | 15 | Balance-sheet/brand durability, industry growth, execution certainty |
| Capital investment / project certainty | 15 | Capex size + how locked-in (incentive-backed, under construction) |
| MF / townhome demand relevance | 15 | Urban/inner-suburban fit, income tier match to Class-A / for-sale TH |
| Repeat momentum | 5 | Reinforcement of a market across reports (new deals score low; repeated/gaining-momentum deals score higher) |

**Score sensitivity to data quality:** Octapharma's move from a narrower, contested county vote to a unanimous city-council approval removed most of its remaining project-certainty discount (+3); JetZero's non-binding financing Letter of Interest was deliberately not scored as a positive, since "explores financing" carries none of the certainty of an actual commitment; Citigroup's promotion from Excluded to Ranked reflects a concrete in-window hiring-ramp fact overriding a prior "pre-window commitment" framing.
